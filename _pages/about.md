Welcome! I’m **Zhuohan Fang**, an Analyst at The Lantau Group in Shanghai, where I focus on China’s power sector and electricity market reforms.  
I hold a Master of Public Policy from UC San Diego’s School of Global Policy and Strategy, and a Bachelor's degree in Agricultural Economics from Zhejiang University.

My research interests lie broadly in **international trade**, **environmental economics**, and **industrial policy**, particularly in how global value chains (GVCs) intersect with clean energy transition.  
I'm currently applying to PhD programs for Fall 2026 in public policy, political science, and environmental & resource economics.

Previously, I worked on research projects related to:
- Clean energy commodity trade (eg, solar panels and wind turbines) in the Indo-Pacific region under an international political economy context
- Urban environmental degradation and adaptation, and residential electrification cost mitigation in Southern California
- China's self-reliance industrial and innovation policy in "bottleneck" technology fields, like semiconductor and battery storage

Feel free to explore my research, CV, and writing samples through the menu above. Thanks for visiting!
